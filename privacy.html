Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Seattle Sim Center ("we," "us," or "our") operates seattlesimcenter.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only.

Information We Collect

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to give us through the contact form on this Site: your name, email address, the program you're interested in, and any message you write. We don't require an account, and we don't collect payment information through this Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry — to answer your question, schedule a session, or follow up about a program you asked about. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't add you to a marketing list without your separate, explicit consent.

Cookies & Tracking

This Site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or any other tracking technology. We don't track your browsing behavior on this Site or across other websites.

Third-Party Services

Two outside services are involved in running this Site:

  • Google Fonts — the Site loads typefaces from Google's font service. Loading a font file sends your device's IP address to Google as part of that request; Google does not receive any information you type into the contact form.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — when you submit the contact form, your message is processed by our hosting provider's infrastructure (AWS) and delivered to us by email. AWS does not use this information for its own purposes.

We do not use any other third-party services, embeds, or trackers on this Site.

Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your contact form submission goes to Seattle Sim Center only.

Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain basic business records, after which they're deleted.

Your Privacy Choices

Regardless of where you're visiting from, you can always ask us what information we have about you or ask us to delete it, by emailing fly@seattlesimcenter.com. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of in that regard.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at fly@seattlesimcenter.com.
