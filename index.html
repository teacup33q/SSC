The Experience

Two trainers.
Zero flight hours required.

Every session runs on one of two FAA-approved Advanced Aviation Training Devices, the same class of equipment flight schools use to log real instrument hours, tuned here for an unforgettable first flight.

FAA-Approved AATD

one-G Foundation 172 · 1000

Cessna 172 platform, Garmin G1000 glass cockpit.

  • In-house G1000 avionics emulation, faithful to the real panel
  • Active control-loading yoke with realistic feel and enhanced intercom audio
  • Wraparound 3-screen visual display
  • Wireless connectivity to ForeFlight, WingX Pro & FlyQ
FAA-Approved AATD

Precision Flight Controls CRX

Open-cockpit trainer, built for instrument work.

  • Open-cockpit training environment, built on PFC's ProPanel foundation
  • 430 WAAS-capable GPS for real-world approach practice
  • Full autopilot automation: flight director, HSI & RMI
  • Precision control loading, available with or without motion

Real-World Scenery

Depart Boeing Field, thread the Sound, and land at SEA: both trainers fly on real-world scenery and navigation data.

Live ATC via PilotEdge

Real human controllers on frequency: departure, approach, and tower, exactly like flying in the real airspace.

Certified Instructors

Every session is guided by an experienced instructor, with briefing, coaching, and debrief included.

Logbook-Ready Hours

Both simulators are certificated Advanced Aviation Training Devices: time that counts toward your rating, not just a joyride.

About the Simulators

Under the hood.

The full spec sheet on both trainers, straight from the manufacturers: one-G Simulation and Precision Flight Controls.

FAA-Approved AATD

one-G Foundation 172 · 1000

Model-specific Cessna 172 trainer, engineered in-house by one-G Simulation.

Avionics & Automation

  • 1G-1000 glass panel emulator, modeled on the Garmin G1000
  • Full-scale PFD & MFD display modules
  • 1G-FC700 autopilot with flight director
  • WAAS LPV approach capability
  • LED standby gauges
  • Reversionary-mode engine management with lean assist

Cockpit & Visuals

  • Active control-loading yoke with realistic force feedback
  • Enhanced intercom audio
  • 3-screen wraparound visual display
  • Retractable 27″ LED monitors

Software & Records

  • In-house one-G avionics software & hardware
  • one-G Portal: logs instructor & student hours and lesson records
  • At Seattle Sim Center: portal access to review & replay your lesson data (no visuals)
  • Wireless connectivity to ForeFlight, WingX Pro & FlyQ

Regulatory & Footprint

  • FAA-approved Advanced Aviation Training Device (AATD)
  • Satisfies training & instrument-recency requirements under FAR Parts 61 & 141
  • Built for student through commercial pilots
  • 80″W × 77″D × 58″H, rolls through a standard doorway

Students and instructors can log in anytime to review lesson history, hours, and account details.
